In plain terms

What NORYX does, and what it does not.

Two short lists, so you know where the product starts and where it stops.

NORYX does

  • Find leads from your own Apollo or LinkedIn account, and their verified work emails.
  • Keep them in one pipeline.
  • Write to them by email and on LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram, Telegram and Messenger, from your own accounts.
  • Stop when they answer, and bring every reply into one inbox with the answer written.
  • Research the account before you reply.
  • Show a source for every claim.

NORYX does not

  • Send anything you did not approve.
  • Buy or sell contact lists.
  • Look up an email or phone number without your yes.You press Find email; a miss costs nothing.
  • Ignore the networks' limits.You control the daily caps.
  • Write again to anyone who asked to stop.A "stop" or "unsubscribe" marks them do-not-contact everywhere.
  • Need your CRM, your inbox or a long setup to start.

03 · Reach

Approve the words once.

A campaign is a short sequence: a connection request, a first message and follow-ups, with each lead's name and company filled in. It goes out by email or on LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram, Telegram or Messenger, from your own account.

Launching is your approval

You approve the exact words when you launch. NORYX sends only those, inside the hours and the daily limits you set.

  3. Follow-up

  4. Second follow-up

Trust

Careful in the ways that matter.

Approvals, limits and sources. The unglamorous parts nobody demos, and the parts that decide whether you let a tool write in your name.

unapproved sends
0

Nothing goes out that you did not approve: answer by answer, or through a rule you turned on yourself. A campaign sends only the words you launched, inside your limits.

rule · approvals

credits per contact lookup
1

People search on Apollo costs 0 credits. You decide which contact details are worth a credit, one approval at a time.

rule · credits

brief lines with a source
100%

If NORYX cannot find a source for a claim, the claim does not make the brief.

rule · receipts

  • Isolated by design

    Each workspace is walled off inside the database itself, with row-level security, not just in the app.

  • Credentials encrypted

    Connector credentials are encrypted at rest with AES-256, and no key ever reaches the browser.

  • A record of every change

    Approvals, members and settings land in an audit history your admins can read.

  • You approve what goes out

    A campaign sends only the words you launched, inside the hours and daily caps you set. Answer for me, if you switch it on, works on email and LinkedIn only and says an AI wrote the answer.
